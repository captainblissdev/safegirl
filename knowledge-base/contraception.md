# SafeGirl Knowledge Base — Contraception

## KB-C1 — Adolescent Contraceptive Eligibility

**KB ID:** KB-C1

**Intent:** Contraception

**User question:**
Can I get birth control even though I'm a teenager?

**Answer:**
Yes. WHO guidance states that adolescents can generally use hormonal and non-hormonal contraceptive methods regardless of age. Some methods have specific considerations, so a qualified health provider can help you choose an appropriate method.

**Evidence/claim:**
WHO states that adolescents are medically eligible to use hormonal and non-hormonal contraceptive methods regardless of age. The guidance identifies specific considerations for sterilization and certain contraceptive methods.

**Source:**
WHO Recommendations on Adolescent Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (2018)

**Section:**
Chapter 3, Section 3.3.3 — Medical eligibility criteria for contraceptive use

**Page:**
p. 13

**Conditions/limitations:**
The source identifies specific considerations for sterilization and certain contraceptive methods. This entry does not provide individual method selection or prescribing advice.

**Safety/referral notes:**
Users seeking a specific contraceptive method should be directed to an appropriate health provider.

VERIFIED_KB_CLAIM

## KB-C2 — Parental/Guardian Authorization

**KB ID:** KB-C2

**Intent:** Contraception

**User question:**
Do I need my parents' permission to get contraception?

**Answer:**
WHO guidance recommends that adolescent contraceptive information and services be provided without mandatory parental or guardian authorization, consent, or notification. This WHO recommendation should not be treated as a statement of the specific legal requirements currently applicable in Kenya.

**Evidence/claim:**
WHO states that sexual and reproductive health services, including contraceptive information and services, should be provided to adolescents without mandatory parental or guardian authorization, consent, or notification.

**Source:**
WHO Recommendations on Adolescent Sexual and Reproductive Health and Rights (2018)

**Section:**
Chapter 3, Sections 3.2 and 3.3.2

**Page:**
pp. 10, 12

**Conditions/limitations:**
The uploaded sources do not establish the exact current Kenyan legal requirements concerning parental consent for reproductive health services.

**Safety/referral notes:**
If a user reports coercion, threats, or fear of violence from a parent or guardian, the safety pathway should be considered.

**Evidence status:**
VERIFIED_KB_CLAIM
