# Label review: provisional `expected_class` (2026-10-14)

`resources/eval/retrieval_eval_set_expected_class_PROVISIONAL.csv` is a copy of
`resources/eval/retrieval_eval_set.csv` (unchanged) with two new columns:
`expected_class` and `label_status`. It is generated by
`research/eval/derive_expected_class.py`.

**Rule.** `expected_class` is the class of the acceptable entries
(KB-C contraception, KB-S sti, KB-P pregnancy, KB-G general). It is "none" for
off_topic, gap, held_topic and safety rows, and for rows with no acceptable
entry.

**Result.** 34 rows get a real class (contraception 9, STI 8, pregnancy 5,
general 12). No row's acceptable entries span two classes, so the rule itself
flagged nothing as ambiguous.

The rows below are ambiguous or look mislabelled for a *classifier*
evaluation. In all of them, the class of the entry that holds the answer
(what the rule uses) differs from what the girl is asking about. **None of
these proposals has been applied. They wait for your confirmation.**

The V3 and keyword-baseline predictions are from the 2026-10-14 run
(`analysis_inputs_2026-10-14.csv`) and are shown for context only.
