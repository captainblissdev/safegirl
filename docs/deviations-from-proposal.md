# Deviations from the approved proposal

A register of every material difference between the approved proposal
(August 2026) and what is built on `development` at `cb0b2fb`, checked on
15 October 2026. Proposal wording is quoted from
`docs/proposal-requirements.md` (not tracked in git at the time of
writing), which gives Section 4.2 verbatim and lists the other
commitments by section. Where a justification is not recorded in the
repository, this register says "no recorded justification".

Abbreviations: DL = [`docs/decision-log.md`](decision-log.md);
SG = [Supervisor guidance, 12 October 2026](decision-log.md#supervisor-guidance-12-october-2026);
FRM = [`docs/feedback-resolution-matrix.md`](feedback-resolution-matrix.md).
Requirement statuses are in [`docs/requirements-traceability.md`](requirements-traceability.md).
