# Health terms and numbers new to the KB (22 October 2026)

Step K3, for review. Every health term, drug or method name, number,
age, dose or duration that appears in the **56 approved candidate
entries** and **not in the 21 live entries** (as on `fix/ui-redesign`,
before K2a). Only the text a girl sees is compared: the user question
and the answer of each entry. The four pending entries (KB-C6, KB-C14,
KB-C22, KB-S19) are left out because they are not served.

How the list was made: numbers, ages, durations, doses and percentages
by pattern (every one that does not occur, as a whole number or phrase,
in the live text); terms by hand from the words the new entries use
and the live ones do not (548 such words, most of them everyday
English). Each row gives the first sentence in the entry that uses the
term.

## Points that look worth a second look

These are observations for the review, not changes: no KB text is edited.

1. **Return to fertility after the injection: three figures.** KB-C5 and
   KB-C21 say "up to 12 months after the injection"; KB-C9 says "It can
   take four months or longer". Both may be in FP7 (open question 4 in
   the medic's file lists differing figures), but a girl reading both
   gets two numbers.
2. **Age range for the combined pill (KB-C18):** "from the first period
   to 40 years". The medic's open question 2 records an age conflict in
   FP7 (any method for adolescents, p. 25; 18 to 45 for progestin-only
   injectables, p. 97); KB-C18 also says nulliparous women can use the
   injection without stating an age.
3. **PrEP age (KB-S7):** "not for adolescents under 15", next to KB-S6's
   HPV vaccine for "girls aged 9 to 14". Different products, but the two
   ages sit close together for the same reader.
4. **Breast cancer wording (KB-C20):** a family history of breast cancer
   is listed as "category 1" (no restriction) for the combined pill. The
   entry's own note asks a clinician to review this wording, and
   "category 1" is provider language.
5. **Held-topic overlap (KB-P9):** its question ("I think I am
   pregnant…") is matched by held-topic group C, so tapping it in the
   Topic list gives the held-topic signpost, not this answer.
6. **Provider terms a girl may not know:** nulliparous (KB-C18,
   explained in brackets), DMPA (KB-C5, in brackets), reactive (KB-S11),
   category 1 (KB-C20), U=U (KB-S9, explained).
